## What is the release date and plot for The Marked Woman?

For thriller fans who want a firm release date, Netflix is scheduling **June 5, 2026**, as the day ***The Marked Woman*** arrives globally. The narrative begins under grim circumstances when a routine port inspection in Barcelona turns up a bound and gagged woman locked inside a shipping container. Confronted with a massive wall of silence, the local detectives who take over the case must deal with the fact that the survivor cannot recall her own name.

## Who is in the cast of The Marked Woman?

Bringing the tense Spanish mystery of ***The Marked Woman*** to life is a strong cast of actors who are highly familiar in the region. Candela Peña leads the charge as Detective Anna Ripoll, the dedicated investigator who becomes entirely obsessed with unlocking the secrets behind the nameless survivor. Working by her side is Pol López as Officer Quique Zárate, her loyal partner who must face the growing danger with her.

By bringing in a strong supporting cast, the production adds plenty of depth to the investigation, ensuring that even minor roles feel important as the dark plot slowly unfolds. Acclaimed actor Manolo Solo is set to appear in a prominent role, bringing the kind of dramatic weight that only a seasoned performer can deliver.
