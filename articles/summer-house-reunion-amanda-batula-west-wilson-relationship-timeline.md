## What timeline did Amanda and West confirm at the Summer House Reunion?

Ciara said her alarm bells started on January 17, when she checked Amanda location data and saw Amanda at West apartment. That is why the argument on the ***Summer House Reunion*** kept stretching back before the public statement ever landed. Amanda did not mark that as the start of a romance. Her version was simpler: the bond was close, emotional and still not physical.

The hinge point, according to Amanda and West, came at the end of February. They said their first kiss happened after a brunch around that time, and West said he made the first move. Swooon reported that West had brunch with Kyle Cooke and Jesse Solomon earlier that day. He met Amanda later, which helps explain why that outing became such a fixation once the rumors hit. Amanda described that early stage in direct terms, saying "everything that was happening was PG." That line did a lot of work during the ***Summer House Reunion***.

March 31 is the easy date because that is when the two went public on *Instagram*. The harder question for the cast was what happened just before that post. The gossip threads on *Marie Claire* picked up rumors on March 5, and West denied the relationship again during a March 24 appearance on *Watch What Happens Live*. Amanda confirmed they did not share any physical intimacy until some days after they released their joint public announcement. That is why Ciara treated every earlier denial as part of the story, not as a side note. Before the episode even aired, West had already told *Bravo* there was no overlap with Kyle and that the relationship only became serious in February.

## How did Ciara and Kyle react during the Summer House Reunion?

Ciara was arguing about the pattern more than the calendar. During the ***Summer House Reunion***, she pulled the conversation back to early March and said Amanda had denied anything physical when asked directly. Amanda did not dance around that point. She admitted, "I lied. I 100% lied." That landed harder than any timeline graphic could have.

The roughest moment came when Amanda said she had needed support during a painful stretch. Ciara shot back, "Where was I?" That line got to the real damage faster than the date talk did. Ciara kept coming back to friendship, because this was never just about who kissed first. She said Amanda had been one of her closest friends since joining the show, so the reveal landed as personal, not just messy television. Calling Amanda a snake made that point plain enough for everyone there.

Kyle took some heat out of the exchange when he said the tone toward Amanda had gotten too harsh, even if he understood why Ciara was furious. West had another problem to answer when Lindsay Hubbard asked whether he had been seeing someone else at the start, and he said yes, but not exclusively. Bravo had already set this up as a three part reunion, so nobody on that couch was pretending the issue would be settled in one night. The dates sound cleaner after Part 1, but the friend group still does not. Part 2 airs June 2 on *Bravo*, Part 3 follows June 9, and *Peacock* gets the extended versions the next day.
